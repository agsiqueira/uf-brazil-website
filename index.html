UF in Brazil - Cross-Cultural Engineering and Immersive Technologies for Industry 5.0

UF in Brazil is an eight-week faculty-led program combining immersive technologies, human-centered design, and cross-cultural learning. Make your home base in Praia do Forte and work with Brazilian faculty and students at SENAI CIMATEC in Salvador.

You do not need to know VR to join. Whether you study art, architecture, history, computer science, engineering, or another field, you can learn immersive tools and help shape experiences that connect people with Salvador’s past and present. Industry visits, cultural experiences, and coastal conservation provide context for your projects.

01 / YOUR HOME BASE

Praia do Forte

Live near the coast in shared double rooms at a hostel. Explore the village and shore throughout the eight-week program.

Praia do Forte is home to Projeto TAMAR’s visitor center, a place to learn about sea turtle conservation.

EXPLORE SALVADOR

Listen. Taste. Move.
Pay attention.

Salvador was Brazil’s first capital. Its historic center, including Pelourinho, is a UNESCO World Heritage site. African, Indigenous, and European histories continue to shape the city’s streets, art, food, and music.

01

History & place

Walk through Pelourinho’s streets and ask what a historic building can tell us about the people and place around it.

02

Food & everyday life

Encounter Bahian food traditions through dishes such as moqueca, vatapá, and acarajé—and learn about the people behind them. Curious about Brazilian food before you go? Visit SAMBA Brazilian Kitchen in Gainesville ↗.

03

Music & movement

Listen for the rhythms of Bahia and learn about capoeira, drumming, and dance through local cultural experiences. Want a local introduction to Brazilian rhythms? Explore Bloco GNV in Gainesville ↗.

04

Coast & conservation

From Salvador’s shoreline to your home base in Praia do Forte, consider how coastal life connects community and environment. Take a video look at Ilha dos Frades ↗, a possible excursion destination.

IMMERSIVE EXHIBITS FOR SOCIAL GOOD

Bring the past
into the conversation.

At a historic building in central Salvador, students from different majors will learn to create immersive experiences with Brazilian partners. Projects could bring historical figures and inspiring people’s stories to life, reveal layers of the place’s past, and invite people to engage with the area today.

The work may explore the legacies of slavery alongside Black cultural life and traditions, including religious practices, music, and capoeira. Students will investigate whose stories have been told, whose have been overlooked, and how those histories connect to the present.

Using VR as a kind of time machine is one possibility: visitors could encounter another period or perspective, then return to the present with new questions. The stories and interpretations will be developed through research and dialogue with local collaborators and communities.

  1. Encounter the place
  2. Learn from local perspectives
  3. Design and prototype
  4. Reflect on the experience
