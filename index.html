TWO PLACES. ONE IMMERSIVE SUMMER.

Make Bahia your
classroom.

UF in Brazil is an eight-week faculty-led program combining immersive technologies, human-centered design, and cross-cultural learning. Make your home base in Praia do Forte and work with Brazilian faculty and students at SENAI CIMATEC in Salvador.

Whether you study art, architecture, computer science, engineering, or another field, your perspective belongs here. Industry and research visits, historic sites, cultural experiences, and coastal conservation bring social and sustainability questions into your hands-on projects.

01 / YOUR HOME BASE

Praia do Forte

Live near the coast in shared double rooms at a hostel. Explore the village, the shore, and the region's conservation work while the program unfolds over eight weeks.

02 / THE CITY

Salvador

Encounter historic streets, music, food, and a city whose culture gives the design work its context.

EXPLORE SALVADOR

Listen. Taste. Move.
Pay attention.

Salvador was Brazil’s first capital. Its historic center, including Pelourinho, is a UNESCO World Heritage site. African, Indigenous, and European histories continue to shape the city’s streets, art, food, and music.

01

History & place

Walk through Pelourinho’s streets and ask what a historic building can tell us about the people and place around it.

02

Food & everyday life

Encounter Bahian food traditions through dishes such as moqueca, vatapá, and acarajé—and learn about the people behind them.

03

Music & movement

Listen for the rhythms of Bahia and learn about capoeira, drumming, and dance through local cultural experiences.

04

Coast & conservation

From Salvador’s shoreline to your home base in Praia do Forte, consider how coastal life connects community and environment.

BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Places to learn from

Excursions may include Pelourinho, Balé Folclórico da Bahia, capoeira and drumming workshops, Projeto TAMAR sea turtle conservation in Praia do Forte, Ilha dos Frades, and SENAI CIMATEC’s research and technology facilities.

IMMERSIVE EXHIBITS FOR SOCIAL GOOD

Bring the past
into the conversation.

At a historic building in central Salvador, students will collaborate with Brazilian partners to create immersive exhibits that reveal layers of the place’s past and invite people to engage with the area today.

The work may explore the legacies of slavery alongside Black cultural life and traditions, including religious practices, music, and capoeira. Students will investigate whose stories have been told, whose have been overlooked, and how those histories connect to the present.

Using VR as a kind of time machine is one possibility: visitors could encounter another period or perspective, then return to the present with new questions. The stories and interpretations will be developed through research and dialogue with local collaborators and communities.
