01 / THE PROJECT

Rooted in place. Created together.

Past. Present.
Possible futures.

What if an immersive experience could change how someone encounters a place?

In Pelourinho, UF students from every major and Brazilian collaborators at SENAI CIMATEC Digital e Criativo will work together to research, interpret, and prototype inspiring museum-style experiences. Immersive technology connects the area’s stories with the people who might encounter them.

The project begins with listening: to local perspectives, to the histories held in a place, and to one another. No prior VR experience is required. Research, art, design, storytelling, and engineering all help shape what visitors will experience.

01

The past

Investigate layers of history, including the legacies of slavery. Ask whose stories have been told, and whose perspectives need more attention.

02

The present

Learn from Black cultural life, religious traditions, music, capoeira, and the everyday life of Pelourinho today.

03

The future

Imagine how immersive experiences could support new conversations about memory, belonging, and the future of the area.

WHAT MIGHT YOU MAKE?

A spatial story, an interactive interpretation, or a VR encounter with another perspective. The format is a question to explore together. Themes and outcomes will be shaped through research and collaboration with Brazilian partners; these are possibilities, not finished exhibits or promised formats.

03 / LIFE IN BAHIA

The place is part of the learning.

Live by the coast.
Learn from the city.

Make Praia do Forte your home base. Let Salvador’s streets, people, and cultural life give your work its context.

Praia do Forte

Stay in a hostel in shared double rooms—two students per room. Coastal life and the village become part of your eight-week summer. Nearby Projeto TAMAR offers a window into sea turtle conservation.

Salvador

Explore the UNESCO-listed Historic Centre, including Pelourinho. Learn about Bahian food, music, religious traditions, and capoeira as living practices that help you understand the place you are designing with.

Beyond the classroom

Possible excursions include Pelourinho’s cultural sites, Balé Folclórico da Bahia, capoeira and drumming workshops, Projeto TAMAR, Ilha dos Frades, and SENAI CIMATEC’s research and technology facilities.

Who can apply?

UF undergraduate and graduate students from all majors, with a 2.5 GPA and good standing. The program is not open to non-UF students. No prior VR experience is required.

2027 UNDERGRADUATE FEE$9,487

$395 nonrefundable deposit toward the total fee is due when applying. Remaining fees are due no later than 45 days before departure. Financial aid deferment depends on the amount of eligible aid.

Housing & what’s included +

Housing: A hostel in Praia do Forte, with shared double rooms (two students per room).

The published fee includes: Tuition for six credits, welcome and farewell dinners, all breakfasts, accommodations as part of scheduled program excursions, program-related transportation, international health insurance, and emergency medical assistance.

What to budget for separately +

Round-trip airfare, applicable visa documents, personal travel and expenses, and optional trip cancellation or interruption insurance. Plan for meals beyond the breakfasts and welcome/farewell dinners listed as included.

Financial aid & scholarships +

Eligible financial aid may apply. Contact UF Student Financial Aid and Scholarships to determine your individual eligibility.
